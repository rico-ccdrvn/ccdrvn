About Richard

Your Instructor

I'm Richard Cooke, a certified driving instructor based in Kingston. I teach new drivers going through Beginner Driver Education with our partner school, and private students getting ready for their G2 and G road tests.

Every lesson is built around you. We work on the skills you actually need, I explain the reasoning behind each rule, and at the end of every lesson I go over what you did well and what to focus on next. Two‑hour lessons give us time to get from your door to the right practice areas and make real progress.

Expert panel

I'm on the expert panel for the Ontario G1 practice tests on G1.ca and Tests.ca, where I reviewed the question bank for practical driving accuracy.
